# Priority Policy

## P0 — Critical

Production outage
Critical security vulnerability
Data loss
Major business interruption

Immediate attention.

---

## P1 — High

Important production bug
Critical product functionality
Blocking engineering dependency

Prioritize immediately.

---

## P2 — Normal

Standard feature
Normal bug
Technical improvement

Execute according to roadmap.

---

## P3 — Low

Nice-to-have
Optimization
Non-critical refactor
Documentation improvement

Execute when capacity allows.
