# Decision Making

## Principle

The organization should make the smallest reasonable decision necessary to move work forward while preserving system integrity.

## Product Decisions

Owned by PM.

## Architecture Decisions

Owned by CTO.

## Implementation Decisions

Owned by the responsible engineer.

## Cross-Team Decisions

Owned by Engineering Manager.

## Security Decisions

Owned by Security.

## Cross-Functional Conflicts

Resolved by CEO.

## When Uncertain

Do not invent facts.

Identify:

1. What is known.
2. What is unknown.
3. Available options.
4. Risks.
5. Required decision owner.
