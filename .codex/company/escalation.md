# Escalation Protocol

## Level 1 — Agent

The assigned agent attempts to solve the problem within its authority.

---

## Level 2 — Engineering Manager

Escalate when:

- Multiple engineering teams are affected.
- There are dependency conflicts.
- Work cannot proceed due to another team's task.
- Scope needs engineering coordination.

---

## Level 3 — CTO

Escalate when:

- Architecture must change.
- Technology choice is uncertain.
- A major technical tradeoff exists.
- There is significant scalability or reliability risk.

---

## Level 4 — PM

Escalate when:

- Requirements are ambiguous.
- Scope is unclear.
- Acceptance criteria conflict.
- Product behavior is undefined.

---

## Level 5 — Security

Escalate when:

- Authentication is affected.
- Authorization is affected.
- Sensitive data is involved.
- Secrets are involved.
- A security vulnerability is suspected.

---

## Level 6 — CEO

The CEO resolves:

- Cross-functional conflicts.
- Product vs technical conflicts.
- Priority conflicts.
- Major delivery risks.
- Decisions affecting the overall project.
